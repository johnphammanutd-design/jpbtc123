# Privacy Policy — AI Reply Assistant for YouTube Comments

_Last updated: 2026-10-21_

This policy describes what the browser extension "AI Reply Assistant for YouTube
Comments" (the "extension") does with your data.

## What the extension does

On youtube.com, the extension adds buttons that ask an AI service to draft reply
suggestions for comments. You choose a draft, edit it, and post it yourself. The
extension never posts, likes, or otherwise interacts with YouTube on your behalf.

## Data the extension sends, and when

Nothing is sent until you click one of the extension's buttons. When you do, the
extension sends the data below **directly from your browser to the AI provider
you selected in Settings** (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or DeepSeek), using **your
own API key**:

- **Suggest a reply:** the text and display name of the comment you selected;
  the video title and the first part of the video description; up to 8 other
  visible comments on the page and, if the comment is a reply, its parent
  comment; your Settings (reply language, audience description, preferred tone,
  voice description); and up to 10 replies you recently chose, so the AI avoids
  repeating itself.
- **Scan comments:** the text and display names of up to 30 visible comments, and
  the video title.
- **Pinned comment ideas:** the video title and description, up to 12 visible
  comments, and your reply language and audience description from Settings.

The AI provider processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy:

- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- DeepSeek: https://www.deepseek.com/privacy

## Data stored on your device

The extension stores the following in your browser's local extension storage
(`storage.local`): your API key(s), your Settings, and your 30 most recently
chosen replies. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer
of the extension and is not synced.

## What we do not do

- The developer operates **no servers** for this extension and receives **no data**
  from it.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- No sale or sharing of data with anyone other than the AI provider you chose.
- No remote code is downloaded or executed.

## Your choices

You can remove stored data at any time by clearing the fields in Settings or by
uninstalling the extension. You can stop all data transfer by not using the
extension's buttons.

## Affiliation

The extension is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by YouTube, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, or DeepSeek.
